## 1. How were you invited?

The first screen asks how the study team invited you. The choice decides how you sign in.

![The first screen: QR code or short code, or username and password](/studies-app/images/login.jpg)

| You received | Choose | You are |
| --- | --- | --- |
| A QR code or a short code (5 letters) | **I have a QR code or a short code** | **Anonymous.** No email or password. The app records that you joined anonymously, and the study collects no personal or identifiable data. |
| An email invitation with an account | **I have a username and password** | Signed in with your own account, so the study team knows who you are. |

The researcher sets this up in the Portal. See [Go live](/start/run-your-study/go-live/).

## 2. Scan or enter the code

Allow the camera, point it at the QR code, or switch to the **Code** tab and type the short code.

![Scanning the study QR code](/studies-app/images/scan.jpg)

## 3. Accept the invitation

The app shows the study invitation: its purpose, **your role** (for example *Participant*) and **this device** (*Smartphone*). Tap **Accept**.

![The study invitation with the Accept button](/studies-app/images/invitation.jpg)

## 4. Onboarding, part 1 of 3: learn and allow

A few short screens explain the study. Each has a **Learn more** link. After the introduction, the app asks for what the study needs. Each request comes with a sentence on why.

![Welcome screen of the study](/studies-app/images/welcome.jpg)

In the recording the app asks for these, one at a time:

* **Location.** Android offers *Allow all the time* or *Allow only while using the app*. Collecting in the background needs the first.
* **Physical activity**, for steps and whether you are walking or still.
* **Microphone**, for audio and video tasks.
* **Notifications**, so the app can tell you when a task is ready.
* **Health data** from Health Connect (Android) or Apple Health, such as activity, steps, sleep and vitals.
* **Run in the background.** Android asks you to stop battery optimization for the app, so collection continues when the app is closed.

![The location permission screen](/studies-app/images/permission-location.jpg)

![The health data permission, with the data types the study reads](/studies-app/images/permission-health.jpg)

![Collecting while the app is closed](/studies-app/images/background.jpg)

<Aside type="tip">
You can say no to a permission. The study then collects less, and the Connections tab shows what is inactive.
</Aside>

## 5. Onboarding, part 2 of 3: consent

The app shows the **informed consent** form. Read it and tap **Agree**. Then type your first and last name and sign with your finger.

![Consent summary](/studies-app/images/consent-summary.jpg)

![The confirmation: do you understand the test and consent to participate?](/studies-app/images/consent-agree.jpg)

![Sign with your finger](/studies-app/images/consent-sign.jpg)

## 6. Onboarding, part 3 of 3: done

"Consent saved." Tap **Done**.

![Consent saved](/studies-app/images/consent-saved.jpg)

## 7. Using the app

The app opens on **Home**.

The app has four tabs at the bottom. Your **profile** is the round icon at the top right.

| Tab | What it is for |
| --- | --- |
| **Home** | The study you are in, a summary of what is connected, your progress, and messages from the study team. |
| **Tasks** | Things the study asks you to do. |
| **Statistics** | Your own data in charts. |
| **Connections** | The phone, wearables and services the study uses. |

### Home

![Home: the study, connections and progress](/studies-app/images/home.jpg)

* **About the study** opens the study description, privacy policy, who to contact, who is responsible, and a download of your signed consent.
* **Connections** says how many data sources are active, for example *Partially connected: 5 sources active, 2 inactive*.
* **Your progress** shows how many days you have been in the study and how many tasks you completed and have pending.

### Tasks

Tasks have two lists, **Pending** and **Completed**. Each card shows the kind of task, how long it takes, and how long is left. Tap **Start**.

![The task list](/studies-app/images/tasks.jpg)

| Kind | Example |
| --- | --- |
| **Survey** | A questionnaire with choices, sliders, numbers, dates and free text. |
| **Cognition** | Tapping test, reaction-time test. |
| **Audio / Video / Image** | Record your voice, a short video, or take a photo. |
| **Health** | Health assessments. |

A survey shows its progress (for example *3 of 8*), has a **Next** button, and lets you skip questions the study allows to skip.

![A survey question](/studies-app/images/survey.jpg)

Tasks can be timed. A task that is not done in time moves to *Expired*. Developers: this is the `AppTask` and `UserTask` model in [App tasks](/carp-mobile-sensing/runtime/app-tasks/).

### Statistics

Charts of the data the study collects, for example tasks completed, heart rate, sleep and steps. Which charts you see depends on what the study measures.

![Statistics: tasks and heart rate](/studies-app/images/statistics.jpg)

### Connections

Shows the **phone**, the **devices** you can connect (for example Polar and Movesense, with a **Connect** button), and the **services** the study uses: background sensing, location, air quality, weather and Health Connect.

![Connections](/studies-app/images/connections.jpg)

### Leave the study

Open your profile. **Leave study** stops the study on this phone and takes you back to your other invitations, or signs you out if there are none. **Sign Out** leaves the study and signs you out.
