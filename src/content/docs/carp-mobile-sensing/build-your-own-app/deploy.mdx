**What you'll do:** start the CARP engine on the phone and *deploy* the protocol to it.
A deployment is one participant's copy of the protocol, running on their phone.

There are three ways to deploy. This guide uses the first:

| | Protocol comes from | Good for |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **On the phone** (this guide) | Your app code | Trying things out, single-phone studies |
| **Via CAWS** | The CARP Portal; the app downloads it after sign-in | Real studies with many participants |
| **CARP Studies App** | The Portal; no app code at all | Studies that don't need a custom app |

## Steps

<Steps>

1. **Start the engine.** `SmartPhoneClientManager` runs studies on the phone.

   ```dart
   final client = SmartPhoneClientManager();
   await client.configure();
   ```

2. **Add the study and deploy it.**

   ```dart
   final study = await client.addStudyFromProtocol(createProtocol());
   await client.tryDeployment(study.studyDeploymentId, study.deviceRoleName);
   ```

</Steps>

You'll put this into the app, together with the next step, in [Step 4](/carp-mobile-sensing/build-your-own-app/collect/).
