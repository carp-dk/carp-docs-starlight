A CARP study goes through three steps: **describe** it, **run** it on a phone, and **collect** the data.

| Step | Component | Where |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Describe the study | Study protocol, from the platform-independent domain model | [`carp_core`](https://pub.dev/packages/carp_core) |
| Run it on a phone | CARP Mobile Sensing (CAMS) | [`carp_mobile_sensing`](/carp-mobile-sensing/) |
| Read sensors and devices | Sampling packages: context, survey, health, audio, Apple Watch, Polar, Movesense | [Available packages](/carp-mobile-sensing/sampling-packages/available-packages) |
| Deploy and collect data | CARP Web Services (CAWS), or your own server | [Storage](/start/storage-caws) |

## What happens

1. The researcher writes a **study protocol**: when to collect, what to do, which data.
2. The protocol is either bundled in your app or uploaded to CAWS and given to a participant as a **deployment**.
3. On the phone, CAMS runs the study: it reads sensors and asks the user to do tasks.
4. Each measurement goes to a **data manager**, which stores it on the phone or uploads it.
