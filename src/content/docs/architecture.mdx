A CARP study is described once, run on a phone, and its data is collected.

1. **Describe.** The researcher writes a **study protocol**: when to collect, what to do, which data. The platform-independent classes are in [`carp_core`](https://pub.dev/packages/carp_core).
2. **Deploy.** The protocol is either bundled in your app, or uploaded to CARP Web Services (CAWS) and given to a participant as a **deployment**.
3. **Run.** On the phone, CARP Mobile Sensing ([CAMS](/carp-mobile-sensing/)) runs the study. It reads sensors through [sampling packages](/carp-mobile-sensing/sampling-packages/available-packages) and asks the user to do tasks.
4. **Collect.** Each measurement goes to a **data manager**, which stores it on the phone or uploads it to [CAWS or your own server](/start/storage-caws).

## Inside CAMS

CAMS is one package with four parts: domain, runtime, infrastructure and sampling packages.
