---

CARP is split into a platform-independent **domain model** (`carp_core`), a **client
framework** that runs studies on the phone (CARP Mobile Sensing, CAMS), pluggable
**sampling packages**, and **backends** that deploy studies and store data
(CARP Web Services, CAWS).

## Study lifecycle

```mermaid
sequenceDiagram
    participant R as Researcher
    participant S as CAWS
    participant C as SmartPhoneClientManager
    participant P as Probes
    R->>S: Upload study protocol
    R->>S: Invite participant (study deployment)
    C->>S: tryDeployment()
    S-->>C: Primary device deployment
    C->>P: start() sampling
    P-->>C: Measurements
    C->>S: Upload data (data manager)
```
