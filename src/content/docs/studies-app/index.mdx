The **CARP Studies app** is a ready-made Android and iPhone app. A participant installs it, joins a study, and the app collects the data the study asks for. **You don't write any app code.** You write the study protocol, and the app follows it.

It is built on [CARP Mobile Sensing](/carp-mobile-sensing/) (CAMS) and is open source: [carp-dk/carp_study_app](https://github.com/carp-dk/carp_study_app). More about it on [carp.dk](https://carp.dk/carp-studies-app/).

## What a participant can do

<CardGrid>
  <Card title="Join a study" icon="rocket">
    Scan a QR code, type a short study code, or sign in with an invitation. See [Join a study](/studies-app/join-a-study/).
  </Card>
  <Card title="Give consent" icon="approve-check">
    Read what the study collects, then sign on the screen. You can download your signed consent later.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Do tasks" icon="pencil">
    Surveys, short cognitive tests (finger tapping, reaction time), voice recordings, video and photos.
  </Card>
  <Card title="See their own data" icon="analytics">
    Sleep, heart rate, steps and activity charts, and progress in the study.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Connect devices" icon="heart">
    Health Connect (Android) or Apple Health, plus wearables such as Polar and Movesense.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Collect in the background" icon="random">
    Location, weather, air quality and steps keep being collected while the app is closed.
  </Card>
</CardGrid>

What the participant sees depends on the study. A study with no video task shows no video task, and a study with no wearable shows no wearable.

## Study app or your own app?

| | CARP Studies app | Your own app |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **You write** | A study protocol (and texts: consent, translations, messages) | A Flutter app that uses CAMS |
| **Screens** | Fixed: Home, Tasks, Statistics, Connections | Anything you design |
| **Good for** | Most studies | Custom screens or a custom flow |
| **Start with** | [Run your own study in the app](/studies-app/run-your-study/) | [Your first CARP app](/start/first-app/) |
